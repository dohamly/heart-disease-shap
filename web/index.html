About the Project

Heart Disease Prediction + Explainable AI is a machine learning project designed to predict heart disease risk while making model decisions more understandable through SHAP explainability. It was developed as part of a university AI portfolio to demonstrate end-to-end proficiency in data science, model development, and responsible AI.

Dataset

The Cleveland Heart Disease dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository contains 303 patient records with 13 clinical attributes. It is one of the most widely used benchmarks for cardiovascular disease classification research.

Machine Learning

Three classifiers were trained and evaluated: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost, on a stratified 80/20 split. Logistic Regression was selected as the best performer with an F1-score of 82.9% and ROC-AUC of 0.885.

Explainable AI

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) provides exact feature-level attribution for every prediction. Both global summaries (mean absolute SHAP across the training set) and local explanations (per-patient contribution breakdowns) are available.
